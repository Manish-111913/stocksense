Functional Modules

Everything you need to manage inventory

Engineered strictly for inventory fidelity — avoiding bloated CRM, e-commerce, or banking complexities.

Product Management

Manage products, SKUs, categories, units of measure, and automated reorder levels across catalog classifications.

Warehouse & Locations

Organize multi-hub facility topologies into organized internal storage bays, dispatch staging docks, and temperature-controlled bins.
